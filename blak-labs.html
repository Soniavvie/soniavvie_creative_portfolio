As an intern I edited video, developed ideas, designed social content and supported social listening for clients including Etiqa, Perennial Living and MoneySense.

Etiqa

Award case study, social video, digital out-of-home

Over five months I worked across Etiqa projects from campaign planning through to launch.

For Pet Talks, a pet insurance campaign that let pets "speak" to their owners on social media, I edited the case study video for the campaign's award submission. The campaign drew 80,650 organic video views and engagement five times the industry average.

When Etiqa moved to its new office at Capital Square, I storyboarded, shot and edited A New Home With You, a social post following the team's convoy of yellow scooters from the old office to the new one.

I also worked on Live Ready, Etiqa's brand campaign, which ran on digital screens at Bugis, Beauty World and along the Downtown Line. AI was part of my toolkit throughout, as a creative support for ideas and assets.

Perennial Living

Pitch and launch videos

I led editing on pitch and launch videos for Perennial Living, aiming for an elegant, lifestyle-led story about residents living a fuller life.

Deadlines were tight and turnarounds fast. Staying calm under that pressure earned the team's trust, and with it more creative ownership over the edits.

The full videos are confidential, so stills are shown here.

MoneySense

Animated social posts, WhatsApp sticker pack

For MoneySense's Sure or Not? campaign, which helps people spot investment scams, I took animated social posts from ideation to final artwork, and designed and animated a WhatsApp sticker pack.

Working to tight timelines and changing feedback, I became the team's go-to person for animation, and learned how to make educational content genuinely engaging to watch.

Also at Blak Labs

I worked with Instamedia Labs, the agency's media team, on social listening for ActiveSG. It showed me how media and creative work support each other, and gave me a clearer picture of audience behaviour and how an agency runs a campaign.
